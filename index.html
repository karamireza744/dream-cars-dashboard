How to read this

  • Bold green line = trend: a best-fit line through every sale in the last 3 years (adjusted for mileage where titles state it). "Trend/yr" is its slope; the ± range is a 95% confidence interval. If the range crosses 0, the market is statistically flat. Most cars here are.
  • Yellow line = 3-month rolling median. Faint dots = individual sales. Prices vary ±30–50% by mileage and condition, so single dots are noise.
  • 12-mo % compares the median of all sales in the last 12 months with the 12 months before (n shown). 3-yr % compares the last 6 months with a 6-month window 3 years ago. Blank = too few sales to say.
  • The y-axis is clipped to the 5th–95th percentile of sales. Tick "show outliers" to see everything. Confidence: high 20+ sales in each 12-month window; medium 12-mo % computable; low too thin.
